# Round 2 — moderator brief (read before writing anything)

## What Round 1 established

**Convergence (3 of 5 agents, independently).** DESIGNER (`k`), OPTIMIZER (`z`) and
INVENTOR/GALLEY (`s`) all arrived at the same skeleton:

1. infer roles/priority from features (never ids)
2. linearize into a reading-order stream
3. enumerate a small discrete set of arrangements (archetypes / contiguous
   slot partitions / ¶ | / break patterns) × omission sets
4. for each, bisect ONE shared type scale against exact renderer metrics
   (hierarchy preserved by construction)
5. drop low-priority units when keeping them costs too much scale
6. emit with designer gaps, validate, fallback.

PERCEPTION adds: ink (alpha) boxes instead of element boxes, a render-in-the-loop
pixel critic with thresholds relative to the SOURCE (brand CTA is only 3.2:1),
Crello priors (3,204 wide designs extracted; leaderboard headline ≈ 0.30 of
height, 1–2 lines), dev-time VLM labelling to fit ~25 weights.
JUDGE adds: a 12-invariant output contract + repair pass, a safe-by-construction
floor candidate, the scorer model `Gate × [α·Structural + (1−α)·VLM]`, a
26-size platform sweep (217 unreadable-text cases even for the safe floor,
mostly leaderboard / mobile banners), and the warning that hidden data is
probably generated by a parametric template generator with adversarial knobs.

**Renderer facts now confirmed by 4–5 agents independently:**
- text `opacity` is IGNORED → hide text via colour alpha 0 (incl. every `style_runs` colour); shapes/images honour opacity
- glyphs are drawn at `int(font_size)` → emit integer sizes
- text box height ignored; overflow not clipped; `letter_spacing` drawn but not measured by wrap
- width/height 0 text crashes the renderer; named colours ("white") crash
- rotated corners are clipped at canvas edges even when the unrotated box passes
- transparent padding: bottle ink = 43%×69% of box, logo ink = 40%×72% (and off-centre)
- one render = 27–82 ms native, so render-in-the-loop for top-K is affordable

## Because the core has converged, the "genius" must come from the open problems

1. **Taste without ground truth.** Every search agent's weights are hand-set and
   fragile (GALLEY: one weight flipped two results; OPTIMIZER: keeping the L3
   product hinges on weights). How do we get a principled objective? Ideas on
   the table: dev-time VLM pairwise labels + Bradley–Terry; Crello priors.
   An untested idea for you to attack or develop: **the source design is itself
   a solved instance of the designer's objective** — e.g. inverse optimization
   (fit the energy weights per design so that the SOURCE is the argmin at its
   own canvas among perturbed/alternative layouts, then solve at the target),
   or round-trip/fixed-point consistency as an objective. Is that genius or
   nonsense? Prove it either way.
2. **Topologies beyond rows/columns/stacks:** text over a full-bleed photo (needs
   a calm region), split colour blocks, Z-patterns, multi-product grids,
   collages, overlapping hero compositions.
3. **Extreme targets:** 728×90, 320×50, 160×600, 1080×1920 — what is the right
   behaviour when even the headline can't be legible? Tall/story layouts were
   the weakest outputs for several agents.
4. **Near-ties** (top-10 within 0.03 J) — who decides?
5. **Ink vs box packing:** packing by ink makes JSON boxes overlap — does that
   risk a structural "collision" penalty? Optical margins.
6. **Furniture & decoration:** rotated ribbons always dropped; corner stickers;
   translucent deco circles; bg `cover` is centre-crop only.
7. **Hide policy risk** (opacity/colour-alpha 0 may be judged dishonest).
