## How to run

```
# from the repo root
.venv/bin/python design/debate/bench/assets_gen.py      # once: bench/assets (brand copies + 2 test assets)
.venv/bin/python design/debate/bench/designs.py         # once: bench/designs/*.json + source renders
.venv/bin/python design/debate/bench/run.py --engines proto --workers 9 --timeout 180   # resumable
.venv/bin/python design/debate/bench/run.py --module my_pkg.my_engine                   # any resize()
.venv/bin/python design/debate/bench/rescore.py         # after a scorer change (engines not re-run)
.venv/bin/python design/debate/bench/report.py          # CSVs + this file + sheets
```

Engines: `baseline, safe_floor, optimizer, optimizer_fixed, galley, designer, perception` and, for Round 4, `proto` (= `design.debate.proto.engine.resize`, HIDE_MODE alpha), `proto_zunder`, `proto_nevertext`. Env `BENCH_PROTO_HIDE_MODE`, `BENCH_PROTO_DISJOINT`, `BENCH_PROTO_TOP_K` override proto switches.

## What the proxy scorer can and cannot measure (read this)

Measures well (deterministic, renderer-exact): structural hard checks (ids, schema-ish canvas/z/finite, frozen fields, unrotated bounds, non-cover image aspect, renderer-crash conditions); renderer gaps (pixel-verified hidden-element leaks, int-font mismatch, letter-spacing/line overflow vs the box, text ink off canvas, rotated-corner clipping, visible missing-asset placeholder); perceived text size at a 1024px judge view; glyph-ink collisions excluding group-mates, translucent decor and pairs already overlapping in the source; contrast and busyness under glyphs relative to the source's own values.

Measures crudely (heuristic proxies, weights hand-set, NOT calibrated against a VLM): hierarchy (font-size order only), alignment (shared ink edges/centres), orphaned small text (source proximity partner hidden), fill and dead bands relative to the source, faithfulness as source-ink share kept + 'largest text kept'.

Cannot measure: taste (balance, rhythm, whether a layout 'looks designed'), semantic correctness of role choices (e.g. a product caption promoted to subhead), whether a drop was the *right* drop, brand feel, photo subject cropping under `cover`, and anything a VLM would weigh holistically. Two layouts can both score 100 and look very different — use the contact sheets. The composite is a regression and triage tool, not a ranking of design quality. EVAL_SIDE=1024 and the 9px floor are assumptions.
